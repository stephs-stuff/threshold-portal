Step inside

Your writing lives in a private GitHub repository. The links below take you to GitHub, where you sign in with your existing account. This public page never asks for your password or stores your journal.

Open my private Field Journal ↗How to write an entry ↗

Not signed in? GitHub will ask you to sign in before showing private material. A dedicated, in-page writing interface is a future enhancement; this entrance is the secure, simple starting point.

Two ways to write

  1. Write here, independently. Open the private journal, choose a Quick Thought or Field Journal template, and save a new entry.
  2. Write through conversation. Explore ideas with ChatGPT, review a proposed entry, and explicitly approve it before it is committed.

There is no requirement to reach a conclusion. A good question is already worth keeping.
