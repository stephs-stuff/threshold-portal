Shadows on the Wall

Plato, The Republic, Book VII · translated by Benjamin Jowett

And now, I said, let me show in a figure how far our nature is enlightened or unenlightened:—Behold! human beings living in a underground den...

Plato asks us to imagine people who have lived their entire lives in a cave, seeing only shadows cast on a wall. What they see is real as an experience—but is it the whole of reality?

A question to carry with you: What might we mistake for the whole simply because it is all we have ever known?
