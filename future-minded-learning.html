Future learning project · October 2026

The Future-Minded Learning Architect

Working proposition: Prepare learners to think, adapt, connect, and exercise judgment across multiple plausible futures—not for a single predicted 2040. Preserve the purpose of education; reconsider the methods.

Six areas of study

  1. Cognitive science: attention, cognitive load, metacognition, curiosity, transfer, cognitive offloading.
  2. Human–AI interaction: trust calibration, automation bias, human oversight, AI as tutor, critic, collaborator or substitute.
  3. Strategic foresight: horizon scanning, scenarios, backcasting, futures literacy.
  4. Authentic assessment: competency evidence, oral explanations, portfolios, performance in novel contexts.
  5. AI systems: models, retrieval, agents, structured data, evaluation, privacy and governance.
  6. Change without management: small pilots, communities of practice, evidence and low-friction adoption.

A flexible 12-month pathway

Months 1–3: Study the human learner and map where AI removes productive versus unproductive friction.

Months 4–6: Prototype an AI-supported activity and an assessment of independent understanding.

Months 7–9: Write three plausible community-college scenarios for 2035.

Months 10–12: Evaluate a pilot and document findings, limitations, accessibility and next questions.
